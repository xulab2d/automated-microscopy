GRAPHENE / hBN
Graphene on 90 nm SiO2/Si is the next-dataset default. Both materials have separate
90/285/300 nm conditional optical profiles. The 2DMatGMM graphene prior importer
explicitly converts BGR order and the opposite contrast sign and retains its
foreign optical context. It is a prior, not a calibrated local layer classifier.

Raw saved TIFFs can be prepared with verified chip-domain/background evidence and
explicit optical context, then classified pixelwise. Substrate and unknown have
their own outcomes. Existing parent masks, disconnected terraces and holes remain
separate. Color, edge, texture and size evidence remain independently reviewable.
Useful-area ranking defaults to25 um2; smaller features are retained. Usable size
uses an actual inscribed rectangle in the eroded mask, not an exterior bounding box.
See docs/MATERIALS-GRAPHENE-HBN.md for primary sources, assumptions and commands.

Synthetic 1.05 MP runs took1.07–1.24 s on one CPU thread; a saved bare-substrate
control produced no selected regions. Preparation of one saved5.07 MP raw image
took2.94 s. These do not establish real graphene/hBN precision, recall or thickness
accuracy. There are no independently confirmed thin positives in this dataset yet.
