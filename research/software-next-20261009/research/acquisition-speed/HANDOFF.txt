STAGED ACQUISITION CHANGES — OFFLINE ONLY, NO MICROSCOPE CONNECTION

Implemented
1. Integrated vendor retention v2. Saved AF score/preview/metadata receipts and
   selected raw readback precede CZI release. Probe TIFFs/resume identities stay
   intact. Four pending outputs/512MiB threshold stops further captures; existing
   old/unpersisted jobs are retained. Root integrated the campaign seed hook.
2. FocusResult now includes outcome and budget. Focus routines accept keyword
   max_elapsed_s/max_captures; nested quick/fine fallback shares its caller budget.
   Default local AF is120s/40 images; recovery180s/48. A focus-surface anchor
   shares180s/48 across local+recovery, plus at most one selection and one final
   verified-anchor capture. Options max_anchor_elapsed_s/max_anchor_captures tune
   this measured-focus budget. A late last frame can be saved but never accepted
   as a valid focus result. Budget failures retain saved curve samples and update
   focus-result/recovery status explicitly. STOP and camera exceptions propagate.
3. Sparse discovery defaults to240s/64 total capture attempts, including nested
   focus, plus the existing32 geometric-probe lifetime cap. New keyword controls
   are max_elapsed_s/max_total_captures. Time/capture exhaustion is unresolved,
   with no invented outline/final image. Limits are cooperative; an in-flight
   RPC/cleanup may overrun, so these are not hard realtime motion deadlines.
4. After a resolved coarse focus bracket, a five-point local quadratic plus an
   independent confirmation may replace a longer fine sweep. Unresolved fits
   fall back to the original fine sweep under the same total budget. Short
   fine sweeps of6 or fewer images are unchanged. adaptive_fine=False disables
   this conditional optimization. No peak/texture thresholds were relaxed.
5. ZenMicroscope.capture_with_processing(processor) can run pure CPU work on
   the first raw frame while RunJob finishes. It still returns NOTHING before
   successful RunJob completion and job unload. Pixels are read-only during
   processing. Late errors, STOP and processor exceptions preserve cancellation
   and cleanup; processor-thread hardware calls are rejected without blocking
   external STOP. Focus metrics use this capability when qualified. Default is
   sequential; processing_overlap=True requires exact-source/profile stationary
   acceptance via processing_overlap_acceptance. No asynchronous motion pipeline
   or unverifiable persistent camera binding was introduced.

Future stationary acceptance (NOT EXECUTED)
scripts/qualify_capture_overlap.py defaults to a no-client dry run. Its explicit
--execute mode checks operator hold and lease, performs3..6 stationary pairs,
keeps raw TIFFs, validates same-frame metric equivalence, complete/unloaded jobs,
fixed optical metadata, <=0.3um movement and <=3% RGB repeat variation. It writes
an acceptance only on success.3% is an engineering stability gate, not material
calibration. It requires matching-source offline-protocol-verification.json.
Do not execute while the microscope is being used by someone else.

of saved elapsed intervals, which may include pauses. Saved first-frame tails
are0.111s median and0.230s p90: this is the CPU-overlap ceiling, not0.54s.

Fifteen eligible saved fine curves all pass the five-measured-point fit and an
INTERPOLATED confirmation check, reducing modeled fine-stage images by69.
Independent live confirmation remains mandatory. report.json applies historical
elapsed per recorded edge-focus sample as a conditional time estimate. This
does not establish hardware speedup or a global scan multiplier. The existing
LZW quantitative TIFF format and every planned raster image are retained.
