MODEL EQUATIONS AND UNITS
Stack: air / MoTe2 / SiO2 / semi-infinite Si. Wavelengths and thicknesses are nm.
The crystal c-axis is normal to the surface. epsilon_parallel=epsilon_xx=epsilon_yy,
epsilon_perpendicular=epsilon_zz. With exp(-i omega t), positive imaginary epsilon
represents absorption; choose the decaying kz branch. For q=sin(theta_air):
  kz_s/k0 = sqrt(epsilon_parallel - q^2)
  kz_p/k0 = sqrt(epsilon_parallel * (1-q^2/epsilon_perpendicular))
  Y_s = kz_s/k0; Y_p = epsilon_parallel/(kz_p/k0)
  r_ij = (Y_i-Y_j)/(Y_i+Y_j)
Bottom-up recursion:
  r_eff_j = (r_j,j+1 + r_eff_j+1 exp(2i kz_j+1 d_j+1)) /
            (1 + r_j,j+1 r_eff_j+1 exp(2i kz_j+1 d_j+1)).
R=abs(r_eff)^2; average s/p intensities. Uniform filled-pupil averaging integrates
u=sin(theta)^2 uniformly from 0 to NA_illumination^2. That angular distribution is
an assumption, not a measured aperture. At NA=0 the model reduces to normal
incidence. The 20x objective accepts NA<=0.4; its maximum is not the actual
illumination NA. The model is not an image diffraction/defocus simulation.

For effective spectral weight W_c(lambda)=lamp*objective*camera response:
  I_c(t) proportional to integral W_c(lambda)*R(t,lambda) d lambda
  C_c(t) = 1 - I_c(t)/I_c(substrate).
If the manufacturer curve means QE per incident photon, energy irradiance needs
an additional lambda/(hc); if it is responsivity per radiant power, it does not.
The vendor plot does not define that convention, so both interpretations are
explicit nuisance cases. Channelwise gains cancel this ratio only for linear,
unclipped, offset-corrected independent channels. A power response produces
C_observed=1-(1-C_linear)^gamma; color mixing requires separate calibration.

https://doi.org/10.1021/nn5007607.s001

Biswas et al., ACS Nano (2023), supporting methods:
https://pubs.acs.org/doi/suppl/10.1021/acsnano.3c00145/suppl_file/nn3c00145_si_001.pdf
The authors report initial identification on285nmSiO2 using approximately7%
contrast per layer followed by independent characterization. This is a useful
order of magnitude, not an RGB vector or a universal threshold.

Fang et al., Applied Surface Science605,154813 (2022):
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169433222023418
Spectroscopic ellipsometry covers0.75-5.91eV and100-450K on5-25nm CVD2H polycrystalline
films on sapphire. The thickness dependence is evidence against treating all
MoTe2 thicknesses as one exact bulk dielectric function. This study does not
supply a verified exfoliated monolayer calibration here.

Ruppert, Aslan and Heinz, Nano Letters14,6231-6236 (2014):
https://doi.org/10.1021/nl502557g
Single/few-layer spectroscopy confirms strong thickness dependence and a1.10eV
monolayer optical gap. Near-infrared bandgap evidence alone does not specify
visible RGB optical constants. Phase identity of the current batch is unconfirmed.

An important actual diagnostic: our nominal0.7nm bulk-tensor model with vendor
response/scenario ensemble gives R~.26-.37 and G~.17-.37, appreciably larger than
the above empirical thin-flake reports. Thus neither0.7nm nor a minimum curve
distance can be advertised as1L. Small inferred thickness can reflect model
mismatch, rather than a physically sub-monolayer object. Preserve this discrepancy
instead of tuning spectra to force agreement with an unconfirmed sample.

fit_effective_response_basis accepts known sample/substrate reflectance spectra,
observed RGB contrasts, a small nonnegative spectral basis, separate training
and validation indices and reference provenance. It fits normalized spectral

be circular. Prefer independently measured spectra or independently confirmed
terraces, then compare held-out residuals to the actual noise covariance.

RANKING / UNCERTAINTY CONTRACT
For covariance Sigma, d2=(C-observed-C-model)^T Sigma^-1 (C-observed-C-model).
The single curve returns a profile likelihood-ratio interval using supplied
deltaChi2; the ensemble minimizes over explicit nuisance scenarios. Multiple
disjoint compatible thickness branches are flagged, as are grid-edge intervals.
exp(-d2/2) is a compatibility score, not a calibrated posterior probability.
No invented noise floor is inserted; singular covariance is rejected. Unknown
model discrepancy is not magically quantified by camera covariance. Optional
model covariance must come from independent residual evidence.

For this instrument, use area_px*abs(det(camera_transform.matrix)) for actual
area and retain20-25um2 plateaus individually. A20um2 disk has diameter5.05um;
at550nm/NA.4 the Rayleigh scale is about.84um. Pixels are correlated and edge
mixing is material at this size. Preserve an interior color estimate and full
mask separately; do not add disconnected terrace areas to pass the area floor.
