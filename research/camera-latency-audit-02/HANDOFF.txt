OFFLINE INVENTORY FINALIZATION AUDIT — 9 October 2026

Established cause: the macro succeeded, then ZEN failed to finalize an empty
workflow result set. The saved log explicitly says:
  RunJobAsync(): The job execution has completed successfully
  System.ArgumentException: itemsToBeFinalized must not be empty
Inventory and done JSON were published before the failure. The JSON files are
external macro outputs, not registered workflow result items. The generated
job has empty Tool/Task Outputs. No vendor-result folder remains because ZEN
then discarded results and deleted its sandbox.

This is NOT evidence of a camera readback or profile-verification failure.
Inventory mode returns before verify_profile and never acquires an image.
EasyHDR=true still needs independent semantic clarification before any benchmark;
AxioCamHDR=false and AxioCamNativeHDR=false do not prove EasyHDR is harmless.
Auto-white-balance binding remains unresolved. All benchmark gates stay enabled.

Minimal defensible change proposal, NOT applied:
- In inventory-only client handling, report the saved matching-session
  inventory/done files as collected, separately record RunJob finalization as
  failed, and preserve the original gRPC error. Do not relabel the vendor job
  successful and do not swallow benchmark-mode failures.
- A clean-success job repair requires a documented non-image result registration
  or a supported execution path without result finalization. Neither binding
  has been established. Do not invent an output parameter or take a dummy camera
  image just to hide the finalization failure.
- Benchmark images saved by Zen.Application.Save may also be external to the
  workflow result store. Therefore benchmark finalization success remains
  unproven even if future frame saves succeed.

No prototype, camera profile, production code, job registration, hardware,
service or server was changed by this audit. Further work was superseded by
portable laptop data handoff. Relevant log evidence is alongside this note.
