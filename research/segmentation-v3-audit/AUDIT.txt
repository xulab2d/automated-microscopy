EXACT CURRENT CODE HOOKS

1. analysis.py:tile_region (line27) projects plan.polygon_um into each raw field.
   It uses the planned polygon, not the acquired raster's observed enclosure.
   colors.py:prepare_contrast(line89) erodes that domain by edge_margin_um12 and
   calls imaging.py:background_surface(line43) on all valid pixels. Large
   contamination or mislocated edge/holder support can dominate that fit.
   background_surface checks positivity over the whole field; old failure logs
   show a positive fit on support but negative extrapolation outside it.

2. analysis.py:_refine_objects(line158) constructs binary foreground envelopes,
   picks a strongest seed and GrabCuts each. The repaired implementation retains
   all supported disconnected pieces, which fixed earlier disappearing objects.
   It does not separate connected different-color terraces inside one parent.
   Its foreground is made from median3 + Gaussian0.6 um smoothing, Mahalanobis
   substrate_sigma5 and contrast norm>.015. Source floor is.25 um2, so expensive
   graph cuts and bookkeeping also cover tens of thousands of sub20 um2 objects.

3. staged_features.py:analyze_feature_frame(line141) calls prepare_contrast,
   _refine_objects, measure_existing_regions, _geometry and apply_sieves.
   It does NOT call analysis.py:extract_regions, _boundary_evidence,
   _facet_fraction, published_shape_score or ColorGroups/coherent_region_labels.
   Consequently options such as max_uniformity/min_boundary_support do not
   govern current staged selection. They are present in config but inactive in
   this path. Do not pretend changing those numbers repairs the staged pipeline.

4. staged_features.py:_geometry(line65) preserves exact instance area, holes,
   polygon and stage projection. usable_width is twice the largest distance-
   transform radius. It is not a largest clean rectangular device region.
   apply_sieves(line96) selects area>=100, width>=5 and non-rejected mean local
   signal. Five useful objects with unavailable measurements still remain
   selected for broad review, with explicit warnings. quality_score is the
   Mahalanobis signal capped at50, not flatness, cleanliness, or crystalline
   morphology. Thin prior compatibility uses regional mean/covariance only.

5. measure_existing_regions.py:_measure_one(line52) already erodes interiors
   by0.5 um and excludes every other foreground object plus1 um clearance from
   local substrate annuli(7 um). It measures spatial1 um patches, records
   interior covariance and background sector-systematic covariance separately.
   Preserve this measured uncertainty work. However, it falls back to the FULL
   mask if too little eroded interior remains. That fallback must not silently
   qualify an edge-dominated small terrace as a clean usable region. There is no
   connected uniform-region choice and no inscribed rectangle measurement.

6. thin_contrast.py:thin_contrast_evidence(line21) implements a descriptive
   +R,+G-R directional search prior and broad G interval. It does not establish
   MoTe2 chemistry or layer count. Use pixel/plateau populations for ranking;
   report approximate optical thickness groups, never calibrated layer labels.

7. Useful reusable experiments, with limitations:
   - research/staged-analysis-audit/observed_normalization_v2.py:
     normalize_observed(line22) separates geometry-valid domain from recorded
     SUBSTRATE-only background support, verifies raw/context/labels, selects
     constant/affine/quadratic by spatial held-out error, and leaves off-domain
     values invalid. This recovered31 fields while preserving source geometry.
     It is research-local with chip001 assumptions; refactor the actual helper
     interface rather than importing its script/star-import side effects.
   - coherent_regions.py:coherent_region_labels(line13) already demonstrates
     local RGB GMM + spatial connected components with substrate anchors. Its
     prior full-field output fragmented real features and generated many small
     regions. Reuse connectivity/background concepts, not its output as truth.
   - thin_regions.py proposes block-based plateaus independently of parents;
     its earlier real-data shortlist partitioned bare substrate. Do not restore
     that backend as the detector, even though idealized synthetic tests pass.

PROPOSED COHERENT IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY (AWAITING PARENT GO)

A. Build one versioned chip support atlas from raw registered acquisition
   evidence, recorded boundary labels and measured enclosure. Keep separate
   masks for on-chip domain, observed bare-substrate fit support, off-chip,
   edge margin, unknown and contamination evidence. Color-selected substrate
   support must NEVER be the allowed flake domain: it would delete differently
   colored flakes. A preview mosaic can display these masks, but radiometric
   estimates must come from raw linear RGB, not gamma/JPEG/blended preview.
   Preserve source-to-stage transforms and overlap confidence; present residual
   registration uncertainty rather than claiming pixel-perfect stitching.

B. Normalize against that substrate support, with validated fit and explicit
   insufficient-support states. Hard-veto confirmed off-chip/margin, keep unknown
   separate. Reuse the successful observed-normalization invariants. Do not
   clamp negative fitted values or infer substrate through unsampled holes.

C. Retain broad connected physical parents, then subdivide their pixels into
   connected locally consistent contrast plateaus using RGB covariance and
   image edges. Preserve weak, thin-looking plateaus attached to a thick parent
   even when the parent's global average is bulk-like. Represent parent-child
   ownership explicitly. A plateau must stay connected; do not pool disjoint
   same-color patches or fill substrate gaps. Save parent labels and child
   labels separately with stable versioned IDs and exact pixel masks/holes.

D. Compute independent region evidence: supported boundary fraction/localized
   step, straight-edge/facet evidence, low-frequency slope versus flat plateau,
   spatial texture, saturation, heterogeneous patch spread, debris/halo context,
   clipping/focus evidence, and eroded usable area. Shape and texture should
   improve a transparent ranking with explicit reasons; no single solidity or
   mean-color threshold is a material classifier. Neighboring thin terraces
   must not inherit rejection solely from a dirty/heterogeneous bulk parent.

E. For each connected uniform plateau, measure the eroded interior and its
   largest INSCRIBED all-valid uniform rectangle. The ordinary axis-aligned
   histogram/stack algorithm is O(pixels) and exact for its orientation; if
   optional rotation is used, state sampled orientations/approximation. Save
   rectangle pixel vertices, physical dimensions, area, erosion and uniformity
   criteria. Never use a minimum-area bounding rectangle that includes outside
   pixels, holes or a dirt island. Report no usable rectangle when erosion or
   contamination removes it; do not silently revert to the whole outline.

F. Rank from20–25 um2 upward with a distinct small-region tier(<50 um2), requiring
   stronger local boundary/clean-interior/coherence evidence for that tier.
   Keep broad parent, rejected child and below-priority observations reviewable.
   Keep magnitude/size/quality evidence separate from descriptive material or
   thickness compatibility. Show parent area, plateau area and clean rectangle
   area separately. Avoid claiming probability without real calibration.

  (ix+2*iy)%4 offered only two folds on vertical strips; current(ix+iy)%4 does not.

  attached to bulk; cannot use bulk parent mean or solidity to reject the child.

  anisotropic pixel scale and erosion when computing physical usable area.

  can remain broad physical-feature observations.
- Small tier: synthetic20/25/50 um2 connected plateaus versus textured dust at
