All 35 boundary-label hashes match the acquisition manifest. Of these failures:
- 28/35 (80%) contain recorded substrate, independently visible in raw previews.
- 26 contain more than 1% substrate; two contain only a narrow substrate sliver.
- Seven have zero recorded substrate and visibly show holder only: tiles0,6,90,
  250,272,313,414. These should stay excluded, rather than become analysis errors
  implying missing on-chip analysis.

Two concrete causes are reproduced in normalization-fit-diagnostic.json:
- The acquisition planning polygon includes an outward coverage margin. It is
  not an exposed-substrate mask. Only64.5% of tile1's eroded planning mask is
  labeled substrate; holder contaminates its robust background fit.
- background_surface rejects a polynomial if it is nonpositive ANYWHERE in the
  camera image, including outside the chip. Tile32's original fit is positive
  on every fit-support pixel, but extrapolates to -12190 camera counts off-chip.
- Tile180 is an interior field dominated by a large dark aggregate. Its
  geometric mask is the whole field, so the robust fit is also contaminated;
  independently recorded substrate remains available around the aggregate.

Isolated prototype: observed_normalization.py. It is NOT imported by production.
It requires the completed raster's measured enclosed footprint and matching
recorded boundary-label hash, actual XYZ and camera optical profile. The runner
also verifies TIFF and sidecar hashes against the immutable acquisition manifest.
The evaluation domain comes from the observed enclosure, with the existing12um
edge exclusion. Background samples come from recorded SUBSTRATE labels with1um
clearance. The domain does NOT require substrate color, preserving bright/dark
objects and terraces inside the chip. Off-chip arrays are explicitly invalid.
Constant, affine and quadratic fits are compared using four spatial block groups;
the simplest within one standard error of the best held-out score is selected.
Positivity is checked on the measured analysis domain, never on holder pixels.

Focused check: tiles1,32,180,354 now have positive, visually sensible on-chip
normalization. Four held-out fits favored a quadratic. Tile0 stays excluded;
tile7's tiny sliver also stays excluded after the unchanged12um edge margin.
This is normalization evidence, NOT a recovery/recall claim for all28 fields.
The observed enclosure is a finite-resolution raster boundary; its discretization
and the12um exclusion still sacrifice some chip-edge area. Do not silently treat
every previously failed field as recoverable. The first diagnostic that eroded

that destroys substrate support around classifier holes and is not the repair.

Integration proposal after the active worker is stopped at an explicit boundary:

2. Four thin-reference matches

All four masks outline visible physical features, unlike the user-rejected bare
substrate cards. However, NONE establishes a uniform thin platelet:
- tile69 f-747c06935c43d9,1348um2: long, folded/fibrous-looking feature with highly
  varying interior colors. It is not a demonstrated flat thin terrace.
- tile70 f-ad5a0b9feb5bcf,1759um2: overlapping view of the same extended feature.
  Stage-coordinate projection covers81.9% of tile69's mask. The catalog's
  centroid-based dedup missed this extended-object overlap. Four matches are at
  most three physical features.
- tile111 f-cbd1e4fc5d3302,246um2, and tile107 f-6a923fecfda7c0,114um2: dark,
  blurred compact particles. Their morphology/material/thickness are unresolved;
  neither provides evidence of a thin MoTe2 platelet.

Independent raw-ROI local annuli exclude all saved source instances and require
recorded substrate, without selecting by closeness to zero contrast. These
reproduce the saved mean RGB contrasts. Thus local background misnormalization
does not explain these four matches. No clipping was recorded for these masks.
Raw spatial-patch color spreads are14-24%, while errors of the regional MEAN are
only0.5-2.1%. Only17.7-25.5% of approximately1um interior patches even fall in the
directional R>0,G>R,0.02<=G<=0.45 search cone, before any uncertainty test.
These fractions are descriptive audit measurements, not proposed new thresholds.

Exact failure mechanism: the current sieve tests the significance of the MEAN
color of the entire object. Covariance of that mean shrinks with the number of
spatial samples, even when the object contains heterogeneous colors or a blurred
dark core. A precise mean inside a broad literature direction does not mean the
object has a uniform material terrace of that color. The measured interior
heterogeneity is already saved, but the thin-prior stage does not use it.

Principled later check: keep every broad source-object mask. Within a source
object, measure spatially connected color plateaus using the observed patch
covariance/noise, and compare a constant-color plateau model with a continuous
gradient/mixture model on held-out spatial patches. Apply material-direction
uncertainty to an actual coherent plateau, and apply useful area/width to that
connected support, not to the entire heterogeneous parent. Record color spread,
supported area and focus limitations alongside mean uncertainty. No arbitrary
hard facet/solidity veto, automatic chemical identity, or universal RGB box is
justified by this audit. The broad source geometry remains available even when
thin-reference evidence is unconvincing.

Comparison control: human-rejected-bare-substrate-control.jpg reproduces one of
