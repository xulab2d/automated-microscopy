These ranges collect the nearest thickness in each finite optical scenario, including poorly matching scenarios. They are **sensitivity ranges, not confidence intervals**. Separately, `compatible_layer_intervals` preserves only thickness grid points whose absolute residual satisfies the declared tolerance, including disconnected intervals. A fractional equivalent layer is an optical fit coordinate, not a claim of a fractional physical atomic layer.

For the strongest band, even the most favorable sensitivity scenario predicts RGB `(0.3407, 0.3039, 0.1647)` versus measured `(0.3545, 0.2750, 0.1766)`: measured minus predicted is `(0.0139, -0.0289, 0.0120)`. The model remains too green. Its nearest broad-scenario fit is 4.0 equivalent layers under gamma 0.8, whereas the assumed-linear fit is 3.0. Neither should be presented as an established count. The observed order is useful, but this residual warrants a mismatch label rather than silently fitting away the channel disagreement.

The calculation uses the existing passive multilayer spectral solver with graphene index `n + ik = 2.6 + 1.3i`, conventional spacing 0.34 nm, hash-pinned silicon data and the silica dispersion model. Oxide scenarios are 85/90/95 nm around nominal 90 nm; they are not a measured oxide interval. Illumination NA is 0 or 0.4. Effective response uses the manufacturer Axiocam305 sensitivity trace with equal-energy, 3000 K and 6500 K blackbody spectra and both response interpretations. Gamma exponents 0.8/1/1.2 are unmeasured sensitivity examples, not guaranteed bounds. The grid is 0–60 equivalent layers in 0.25-layer steps. No 285 nm model or external microscope's empirical means are substituted.
