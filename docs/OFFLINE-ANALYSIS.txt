HIGH-RECALL FEATURES AND TERRACES
The original component boundaries and all prior analyses remain unchanged.
The default is to reuse exact checksummed analysis-staged-features instance
masks, IDs and holes; missing or changed masks are explicit failures. Each
parent is partitioned internally into observed RGB/conditional optical groups.
Original parents remain visible even when no child passes the review criteria.
Global foreground resegmentation is an explicitly experimental option only.
terrace_analysis.py writes independent parent and terrace masks. Chip-level
overview geometry and substrate-color consistency determine where normalization
is supported. Off-chip fields and unsupported substrate fits are explicit
outcomes, not silent empty catalogs. Pixel RGB populations / conditional optical
coordinates are split before connected terrace regions are formed.
The chip substrate reference uses up to nine spatially separated fields from
the same recorded exposure/WB/objective/illumination profile. Independently
saved substrate labels determine sampling positions in verified raw TIFFs.
Gross color/intensity mismatch and insufficient support are reported; candidate
colors do not define that reference. This is a conservative QC flag, not a
validated residue classifier or an adjustment of the camera's original pixels.

The review target is25um2 rather than100um2. Edges are trimmed before contrast
measurement. Edge localization, interior uniformity/texture, straight facets,
saturation and focus limitations remain separate evidence fields. Small regions
receive stronger rank penalties. The usable rectangle is the largest all-inside
rectangle aligned with source image axes, a lower bound on the best rotated
device area. It is not the flake bounding box or a claim of crystal cleanliness.

PHYSICAL CONTRAST MODEL
flakefinder/mote2_optics.py implements an anisotropic air/MoTe2/SiO2/Si stack,
NA averaging and RGB spectral integration. The source tensor is Munkhbat et al.
(ACS Photonics2022; https://doi.org/10.1021/acsphotonics.2c00433,
https://zenodo.org/records/6205431). The vendor Axiocam305 sensitivity curves
were extracted from the ZEISS vector PDF, with their provenance preserved.
See research/mote2-optical-model-03/HANDOFF.txt and configs/materials/.

Convention: C=(substrate-feature)/substrate. Darker material is positive.
The camera's earlier exposure ladder was close to linear but lacked a measured
dark reference. White-balance gains cancel only for independent linear,
unclipped, offset-corrected channels. Preview gamma is not scientific contrast.
The effective lamp/objective/camera spectrum remains incompletely measured.
The published tensor is thick2H material, not monolayer-specific constants.
Model ensembles explore declared assumptions; they are not experimental error
bars. Near-zero curve agreement is also compatible with bare substrate.

Use conditional model distance and improvement over the substrate null as
evidence. Unmatched objects remain visible. No current candidate is assigned
a validated layer number. The independent reference checks show that model
agreement alone does not reliably separate all material and residue controls.

REFERENCE CALIBRATION
flakefinder/reference_clusters.py supplies an optical-profile-bound Gaussian
classifier for pixels. It requires at least five distinct confirmed physical
references for each thickness class and bare-substrate controls. Repeated
photographs do not increase the reference count. Provide AFM/Raman/PL records
and separate measured terraces, then evaluate on held-out flakes/chips before
trusting posterior scores. The current batch has no such thickness labels;
the code therefore does not fabricate a trained thickness classifier.

scripts/cluster_flake_crops.py uses pinned DINOv2-small weights locally onCPU.
It consumes candidate crops from the saved feature fields, including rejected
regions >=25um2, retains whole crops rather than truncating them by center-crop,
and writes resumable embeddings and cluster-previews to Drive. Prior analysis
failures/exclusions are counted; they are not claimed as processed candidates.
The standalone review.html displays typical and distant cluster members and
exports provisional cluster labels. A cluster may be mixed. Review members
before propagating labels or training a geometry/cleanliness classifier.

RELATION TO PUBLISHED METHODS
2DMatGMM uses Gaussian contrast classes with a learned false-positive classifier:
https://github.com/Jaluus/2DMatGMM
https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ad2287
Our reference_clusters.py is a separate small-reference Gaussian implementation;
it is not pretrained MoTe2 weights or a claim to reproduce published accuracy.
MaskTerial reports synthetic pretraining and adaptation with5-10 annotated images:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09333
https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlepdf/2025/DD/D5DD00156K
Those images still need genuine instance/layer annotations. No fine-tuned
MaskTerial model or reviewed-clean-interior anomaly detector was trained in this
unlabeled batch. The present deliverable is the auditable segmentation, physical
model, reference-classifier interface and completed appearance-cluster review.
